## 👋 Haloo! / What's Good! 

**Saku Hukum ULM** adalah *platform* super pintar buat ngebantu kamu belajar dan paham hukum dengan gampang, cepat, dan pastinya seru banget! Cocok buat mahasiswa hukum, dosen, atau siapa aja yang pengen melek hukum tanpa pusing. 🧠💡

*Saku Hukum ULM is a super smart platform built to help you learn and understand the law easily, quickly, and in a really fun way! Perfect for law students, lecturers, or anyone who wants to get legally literate without the headache. 🧠💡*

---

## 🔥 Fitur Keren Kita / Our Awesome Features

<br>

### 🤖 Chatbot Jaksa (*Your AI Bestie*)
> **Punya pertanyaan hukum tapi bingung nanya siapa? Tanya Jaksa aja!** 
Asisten AI pintar kita siap diajak diskusi soal hukum kapan aja, di mana aja. Nggak ada lagi drama kebingungan nyari referensi! 💬✨  
*Got a legal question but don't know who to ask? Just ask **Jaksa**! Our smart AI assistant is ready to discuss legal topics anytime, anywhere. No more drama finding references! 💬✨*

### 📖 Kamus Pasal (*Article Dictionary*)
> **Tinggalkan buku tebal yang berat itu!** 
Kita punya database pasal hukum yang gampang banget dicari plus penjelasannya yang *simple* dan *to-the-point*. 📚🔍  
*Leave those heavy books behind! We’ve got a super searchable legal article database complete with simple, to-the-point explanations. 📚🔍*

### 🎯 Pusat Latihan (*AI Practice Center*)
> **Uji seberapa tajam insting hukum kamu!** 
Cobain berbagai simulasi kasus yang seru, mulai dari Korupsi (Tipikor) sampai *Cybercrime* (ITE). AI kita bakal langsung kasih *feedback* dan nilai analisis kamu. 🏆⚖️  
*Wanna test how sharp your legal instincts are? Try out our fun case simulations, from Corruption to Cybercrime! Our AI will instantly grade and give feedback on your analysis. 🏆⚖️*

### 🔀 Alur Perkara (*Interactive Flowcharts*)
> **Prosedur hukum divisualisasikan!** 
Pusing sama teori hukum acara? Tenang! Kita visualisasi-in semuanya pakai *flowchart* interaktif yang gampang dipahami, dari tahap awal penyidikan sampai putusan hakim! 🗺️🔨  
*Legal procedures, visualized! Confused by procedural law theory? Chill! We visualize everything using interactive flowcharts that are super easy to follow, from initial investigation to the judge's verdict! 🗺️🔨*

### 📚 Glosarium (*Legal Glossary*)
> **Paham bahasa 'alien' hukum!** 
Bahasa hukum kadang suka bikin pusing kepala, kan? Cari aja istilah yang bikin bingung di Glosarium kita. Penjelasannya pakai bahasa manusia biasa kok! 🗣️✨  
*Understand the legal 'alien' language! Legal jargon can be a headache, right? Just look up confusing terms in our Glossary. The explanations are in plain, human language! 🗣️✨*
