## HEADLINE FINDING (read first)

> **There is no dedicated "Kejaksaan" / prosecutor track, program, class or specialisation at Fakultas Hukum ULM.**
>
> FH ULM's undergraduate law degree (S1 Hukum) has a single curriculum with a *Program Kekhususan* (PK, concentration) chosen in the later semesters. The prosecutor career is served indirectly by two of those concentrations — **PK Hukum Pidana** (substantive criminal law) and **PK Hukum Acara** (procedural law) — plus extracurricular activity in the moot-court community (Komunitas Peradilan Semu) and internships. Becoming a *jaksa* in Indonesia happens **after** graduation, through the national CPNS/Kejaksaan RI recruitment and the Kejaksaan education programme (PPPJ), not through a university track.
>
> Sources for the concentration structure: <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/sejarah-fakultas/>, <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/bagian-hukum/pidana/>, <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/bagian-hukum/acara/>, <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/formulir-peminatan-untuk-mahasiswa-angkatan-2021/>
>
> What FH ULM *does* have that is prosecutor-specific is **institutional partnership with Kejaksaan RI** — most notably being chosen in 2026 as the first and only campus in Indonesia to host the Kejaksaan Agung's *Adhyaksa Chamber* blueprint seminar. See §3 and §10.

### 2.3 Vision, Mission, Objectives — S1 Hukum

Source: <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/program-studi/hukum-program-sarjana/> (modified 11 September 2026). Original Indonesian preserved.

**VISI**
> "Menjadi pusat pengembangan ilmu hukum yang unggul dalam bidang hukum lingkungan dan hukum adat wilayah tropis lahan basah, berbasis pada nilai-nilai kearifan lokal Kalimantan dan berorientasi pada keadilan ekologis serta daya saing global"

**MISI**
1. Menyelenggarakan pendidikan hukum yang bermutu, relevan, dan kontekstual terhadap dinamika hukum nasional dan lokal berbasis nilai-nilai kearifan lokal dan keberlanjutan lahan basah.
2. Mengembangkan riset hukum yang inovatif dan aplikatif dalam bidang hukum lingkungan, hukum adat, dan hukum agraria khas wilayah tropis.
3. Melaksanakan pengabdian kepada masyarakat secara berkelanjutan untuk meningkatkan literasi hukum dan memperkuat akses keadilan masyarakat lokal.
4. Meningkatkan kualitas tata kelola program studi secara transparan dan akuntabel dengan prinsip tata kelola yang baik.
5. Memperkuat jaringan kemitraan akademik dan profesi hukum, baik di tingkat nasional maupun internasional, guna memperluas cakrawala ilmiah mahasiswa dan dosen.

**TUJUAN (1 of 5, verbatim)**
> "Menghasilkan lulusan hukum yang berdaya saing, memiliki integritas tinggi, berkarakter **WASAKA (waja sampai kaputing)**, dan mampu beradaptasi di dunia profesi hukum."

**Interpretation for a prosecutor-focused applicant:** the programme's declared identity is **environmental law, adat (customary) law and wetland/tropical-region law** — not criminal justice or prosecution. Criminal-law strength exists at department level (§3.3) but the flagship identity is ecological.

## 3. Prosecutor Track / Specialisation (Kejaksaan)

### 3.1 The direct answer

**No prosecutor track exists.** FH ULM offers one S1 Hukum degree. Late in the programme students choose a **Program Kekhususan / Konsentrasi (peminatan)**. Nothing in the published curriculum, programme description, or faculty structure is labelled Kejaksaan, jaksa, penuntutan, or prosecution.

Evidence:
- Programme list: only S1 Hukum, S2 Hukum, S2 Kenotariatan, S3 — <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/program-studi/>
- Department list: only Pidana, Perdata, Tata Negara, Acara — <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/bagian-hukum/>
- Concentration list: 5 PK, none prosecution-specific (see §1.2 table) — <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/bagian-hukum/perdata/>
- Peminatan selection form (angkatan 2021): Hukum Tata Negara, Hukum Perdata, Hukum Acara, Hukum Pidana, Hukum Bisnis — <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/formulir-peminatan-untuk-mahasiswa-angkatan-2021/>
- Full Kurikulum 2020 course table contains no Kejaksaan-titled course — <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/SKS.pdf>

### 3.2 How the peminatan is chosen

Students select a concentration by filling a Google Form circulated by the faculty; the 2021-cohort form is published on the faculty site with one link per concentration. Source: <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/formulir-peminatan-untuk-mahasiswa-angkatan-2021/>
The concentration is taken up in the higher semesters, alongside the *mata kuliah wajib program kekhususan* and PK electives, culminating in a Skripsi within the chosen field. Source: <https://fh.ulm.ac.id/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/SKS.pdf>

**[NOT FOUND]** No published GPA threshold, quota per concentration, or competitive selection criteria for peminatan. **[NOT FOUND]** No published form for cohorts after 2021 — assume the process repeats each year and is announced internally.

### 3.8 Realistic route to becoming a *jaksa* via FH ULM

1. Enter S1 Hukum (§4).
2. Take **PK Hukum Pidana** (or Hukum Acara) at the peminatan stage; prioritise Sistem Peradilan Pidana, Hukum Pembuktian, Hukum Penitensier, Kriminologi, Viktimologi.
3. Join **Komunitas Peradilan Semu** and compete in moot court; use Gedung Desmond J. Mahesa facilities.
4. Do internships — push for a Kejaksaan Negeri/Tinggi placement, and use LKBH/Posbankum for live case exposure.
5. Graduate S.H., then apply through **national Kejaksaan RI / CPNS recruitment** and complete **PPPJ** (Pendidikan dan Pelatihan Pembentukan Jaksa). **[NOTE]** This final step is national policy, outside FH ULM's control, and was not sourced from ULM materials.

### A.2 The most consequential unresolved question

**Which 11 lost their professorships, and are the current "Prof." titles at FH ULM valid?**

On **25 October 2023** — eight months *before* the Tempo investigation — ULM inaugurated **10 guru besar at Fakultas Hukum in a single ceremony**, described by Rektor Ahmad Alim Bachri as unprecedented in ULM's 65-year history: *"belum pernah sebelumnya dikukuhkan guru besar 10 orang sekaligus bahkan spesial lagi hanya satu fakultas."* In the preceding year ULM produced **48 new professors**, reaching **116** in total, the most in Kalimantan.

The 10 were named as: **Prof. Achmad Faisal, Prof. Ahmad Saufi, Prof. Suprapto, Prof. Rahmadi Usman, Prof. Djoni Sumardi Gozali, Prof. Ichsan Anwary, Prof. Anang Shophan Tornado, Prof. Diana Haiti, Prof. Mulyani Zulaeha, Prof. Mispansyah.**
Source: <https://kalsel.antaranews.com/berita/392133/kukuhkan-10-guru-besar-fakultas-hukum-ulm-cetak-sejarah>

**All ten of those names appear on FH ULM's current staff pages, still carrying "Prof."** (see §6.2–6.5), and several hold senior office (Wakil Dekan posts).

**[CRITICAL — UNVERIFIED INFERENCE]** No retrieved source explicitly names the 11 whose professorship SKs were cancelled. The overlap between the October 2023 mass inauguration at the Faculty of Law and the July 2024 finding against "11 dosen Fakultas Hukum" is striking, but it is an **inference, not a documented fact.** It is equally possible that titles were restored after remediation, or that the faculty pages display pre-revocation titles.

**Do not rely on any individual "Prof." title at FH ULM without checking the current SINTA and PDDikti records for that person.** This is the single most important verification step for anyone assessing this faculty's academic standing.
