## Post-upload verification log

### 27 August 2026 — official current leadership

The official FH ULM leadership page confirms the current office labels and names shown in the existing source record: **Hj. Erlina** (Dean), **Saprudin** (Vice Dean for Academic Affairs), **Suprapto** (Vice Dean for General Affairs and Finance), and **Anang Shophan Tornado** (Vice Dean for Student Affairs and Alumni). This confirms that the uploaded report’s “Mulyani Zulaeha / Diana Haiti” entry is historical rather than a current leadership listing. The site will not present the historical names as incumbents.

Source: [FH ULM — Pimpinan](https://fh.ulm.ac.id/profil/organisasi-fakultas/pimpinan/), checked 27 August 2026.

### 27 August 2026 — Hukumonline Corner

ULM’s official 1 August 2024 news item confirms that the FH ULM–Hukumonline collaboration created the **first Hukumonline Corner in Kalimantan** and describes it as providing full access to the Hukumonline legal database for the academic community. The item states that the resource is intended to support student work, lecturer research, and following current Indonesian legal regulations. This supports a carefully limited facilities-page addition; it does not establish individual entitlement, operating hours, or remote-access conditions.

Source: [ULM — Hukumonline Corner](https://ulm.ac.id/id/2024/08/01/ulm-miliki-hukumonline-corner-pertama-di-kalimantan-permudah-akses-literasi-dan-referensi-hukum-terpercaya/), checked 27 August 2026.

### 27 August 2026 — current S1 Law programme accreditation

The official FH ULM announcement confirms the S1 Law programme’s rank of **Akreditasi Baik Sekali**, Decision No. **6691/SK/BAN-PT/Ak/S/VI/2026**, dated 2 June 2026, with a reported score of 350. This is programme-level information, not a statement of ULM-wide institutional accreditation. The report’s account of institutional-accreditation history remains a disclosure item rather than current site copy.

Source: [FH ULM — S1 Law accreditation announcement](https://fh.ulm.ac.id/2026/06/05/prodi-s1-hukum-fh-ulm-raih-akreditasi-baik-sekali-dari-ban-pt/), checked 27 August 2026.

### 27 August 2026 — PPPJ training and appointment

An official Badiklat Kejaksaan RI announcement dated 25 June 2026 confirms that 503 participants in PPPJ Angkatan LXXXIII Gelombang I were appointed as **Ajun Jaksa** at the closing of their training. It supports using PPPJ as a high-level formation step in the career-page timeline and emphasising integrity, professional competence, and continued learning. It does not establish fixed recruitment requirements for any later intake, so GPA, age, marital-status, health, and height thresholds remain excluded from the site.

Source: [Badiklat Kejaksaan RI — appointment of PPPJ 2026 participants](https://badiklat.kejaksaan.go.id/berita/s/jaksa-agung-lantik-503-jaksa-baru-burhanuddin-saya-butuh-jaksa-4c2d6), checked 27 August 2026.
