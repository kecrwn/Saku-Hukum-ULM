### ULM History — Verified Milestones

ULM’s official history states that the Preparatory Committee inaugurated Universitas Lambung Mangkurat on **21 September 1958**, with Lt. Col. H. Hasan Basry appointed as its first President (the former term for rector). It began with four faculties, including **Fakultas Hukum**. The university was then inaugurated as a public university on **1 November 1960** under Government Regulation No. 41 of 1960, again including the Faculty of Law among its four faculties. The official narrative further states that the Faculty of Law began offering the study sections of **Hukum Tata Negara**, **Hukum Pidana**, and **Hukum Perdata** in 1971.

Source: [Official ULM — Sejarah](https://ulm.ac.id/id/history/), accessed 27 August 2026.

## Accreditation

The official FH ULM accreditation page states that the **Program Studi Hukum, Program Sarjana (S1)** holds the BAN-PT rank **“Akreditasi Baik Sekali”** under Decision No. **6691/SK/BAN-PT/Ak/S/VI/2026**, valid from **2 June 2026 through 2 June 2031**. A separate official announcement describes the decision as dated 2 June 2026 and reports a score of 350. This is the current S1-programme accreditation information used in the website.

The same page displays an earlier ULM institutional accreditation decision valid from 20 March 2024 to 20 March 2029. Because institutional accreditation status may change separately from programme accreditation, the website will present the verified S1 Hukum status prominently and link to the faculty accreditation page for the wider record rather than characterizing an institutional status as current beyond the date shown.

Sources: [Official FH ULM — Akreditasi & Sertifikasi](https://fh.ulm.ac.id/profil/akreditasi-dan-sertifikasi/); [Official FH ULM — S1 Hukum accreditation announcement](https://fh.ulm.ac.id/2026/06/05/prodi-s1-hukum-fh-ulm-raih-akreditasi-baik-sekali-dari-ban-pt/), accessed 27 August 2026.
